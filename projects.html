01 · COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS

Percolation & network modelling

This project explored site and bond percolation through computational modelling, with a focus on connectivity, cluster formation and the algorithmic structure of percolation simulations.

Overview

Percolation models provide a simple but powerful way to study how large-scale connected structure emerges from local randomness. I used computational methods to model occupation and connectivity on a lattice, and to analyse how clusters form as occupation probability increases.

What I did

I implemented percolation simulations and used them to study the emergence of connected components across a lattice. The work focused on the computational representation of sites, bonds and neighbour relationships, as well as the behaviour of clusters under random occupation.

Technical focus

The project involved simulation design, randomised occupation order, neighbour detection, cluster connectivity and computational analysis of network behaviour. It also informed the animated lattice visual used on this website’s homepage.
